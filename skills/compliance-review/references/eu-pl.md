# Poland and EU pack

Verified in September 2026 against published legal commentary. The text of the laws is on ISAP (isap.sejm.gov.pl) for Poland and EUR-Lex for EU law. Confirm the current wording before relying on this.

## Poland: consent for marketing messages

- Article 398 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej, in force since 10 November 2024) requires prior consent for commercial information and direct marketing sent by email, SMS or MMS, and phone. It replaced article 172 of the former Telecommunications Law and article 10 of the Act on Providing Services by Electronic Means.
- Consent must be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous. Commentators recommend a separate consent for each channel.
- Having a phone number or email address in a database is not consent.
- Purely service or technical messages (for example confirming a booking you made) are treated differently from marketing. Do not dress marketing up as a service message.
- Do not assume a "soft opt-in" for existing customers in Poland. The sources reviewed describe prior consent as the rule. Ask a Polish lawyer.
- Enforcement is by the President of the Office of Electronic Communications (UKE), who can impose fines. Check the current amounts.

## EU: GDPR

- Marketing needs a lawful basis, and where the national ePrivacy rules require consent, consent is the basis. Give a privacy notice (articles 13 and 14).
- People have a right to object to direct marketing at any time (article 21), including profiling related to it. Honor it and stop.
- Data concerning health is a special category (article 9). See `health-pl.md` for what this means for message content.

## Other EU countries

The ePrivacy Directive is implemented nationally. Some countries allow marketing to existing customers for similar products without new consent, if an easy opt-out is given (often called a "soft opt-in"). Rules differ, so check the country of the recipients.

## Questions for a lawyer or data protection officer

1. Does the consent collected at signup cover these messages, separately for email and SMS?
2. What is the lawful basis for the segmentation and any profiling we use?
3. Do tracking pixels and tracked links need extra information or consent?
4. How do we store and apply unsubscribe and suppression lists, and for how long?
