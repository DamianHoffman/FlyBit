# Changelog

## 0.2.0

First public release.

- Skill `new-automation`: guided builder for small-business email and SMS automations, with playbooks for a welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase and review request, win-back, and lead nurture.
- Skill `compliance-review`: universal checklist and reference packs for the EU and Poland, the United States, and health communication in Poland.
- Skill `sms-check`: counts SMS segments and detects GSM-7 vs Unicode for any alphabet.
- Skill `ab-test`: sample-size planning and significance test for two variants.
